Why AURA Stands Out

🎯 Ground Intelligence

Heavy AI processing happens on powerful ground stations, not on the drone. This enables using larger models without expensive onboard hardware.

👤 Human-Centric

The pilot remains in complete control at all times. AI provides assistance, recommendations, and alerts—never replaces human judgment.

💰 Cost-Effective

Reuses existing FPV drones, avoids expensive onboard AI computers, relies on open-source software. Total cost: ₹5,000-10,000.

⚡ Performance

Extended flight time due to reduced drone weight and power consumption. Better video enhancement improves AI accuracy.

🔧 Modular Design

Easily adapt software to different drones and robotic platforms with minimal changes. Scalable and future-proof architecture.

📊 Real-Time Intelligence

Live object detection, tracking, confidence scores, warnings, and system status—all on a single intuitive dashboard.
