Hardware & mapping

The fixed analog camera reaches the laptop through a compatible USB video receiver/capture device. MAVLink reaches the laptop separately over your ELRS telemetry link.

Before object GPS can appear

  1. Confirm the flight controller and firmware; configure its MAVLink endpoint.
  2. Calibrate the camera lens and fixed mounting angles using the actual captured video.
  3. Survey the ground elevation and flat area; measure video/telemetry delay.
  4. Validate estimates against known ground points before relying on the map.

The current method estimates a box's bottom-centre on flat ground. It cannot promise exact GPS, handle unknown sloping terrain or infer a hidden person's feet. Recordings are never paired with today's aircraft GPS.
